AffineInvariantForge — AI-Synthesized Inductive Safety Proofs

Notes / Description (963/1000)

AffineInvariantForge synthesizes inductive safety proofs for affine integer loops. AI invents invariants and rational implication witnesses; validators independently check exact initialization, simultaneous-step preservation and safety identities against the immutable model. Confidence and success labels cannot replace these checks.

Permissionless synthesis appends root-bound proofs with bounded checker-guided repair. Stale attempts, nonce replay, forged witnesses and cross-problem theorem reads fail closed. No graph heads, funds, privileged finalizers or consumable certificates exist.

Live counter and transfer proofs passed; harder lockstep synthesis failed closed and remains disclosed. The repository includes 43 local tests, an independent BigInt checker, finalized receipts and disclosed synthesis failures. The theorem concerns exactly the supplied mathematical model—not deployed software correspondence, termination or external service delivery.
